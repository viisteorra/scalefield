ScaleGen — generative model on ScaleField v1 latents.

The picture is not H×W×3. It is a PrimeField coefficient vector c
(independent prime DCT grids, N = sum p², default through 31 → 3359).

Encode: image → c  (least squares on the field)
Generate: z ~ tiny prior → c = μ + B z → raster at any res
Transform: do linear / Euler / mix on z or c, then decode

Prior is a PCA Gaussian on the empirical cloud of c from stills.
With ~120 training images, rank ≤ 119. That is the generative
capacity — morphs of the learned cloud, not ImageNet.

Do not put a U-Net or pixel diffusion in here. Work in c.
